Buryad Ugenuud Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

1. How the app works

Buryad Ugenuud is a peer-to-peer language collaboration app. It does not use a publisher-operated account server or central database for user profiles or language content. The app stores data on your device and replicates relevant data directly between participating devices over the peer-to-peer network. Network connections between peers use encrypted transport, but content replicated to a language or account is available to other authorized participants in that network. Do not treat shared language content as private.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle:

  • Profile information: a nickname and, if you choose, a profile image. Nicknames are limited to 40 characters. Profile images can be up to 2 MiB; a smaller image of up to 80 KiB is used in language member profiles.
  • Account and device information: cryptographic account and device keys, language membership and language keys, device name and platform, and pairing and authorization records. Pairing codes contain device and account identifiers and expire after 24 hours. Account data, including the account signing key used for actions, is replicated to devices paired to that account.
  • Language and contribution information: language names and descriptions, translations and descriptions, poll questions and options, votes, comments, emoji reactions, and the associated author/account identifiers and timestamps. Language administrators can view member information for their language.
  • Images and videos you submit: you may select images or videos for profile avatars, polls, comments, and translations. The app stores submitted media in the relevant replicated content. Media may contain embedded metadata, such as location or camera details, so review files before sharing them.
  • Translation-service configuration: a language administrator can enter Google Cloud Translation and Yandex Cloud Translate credentials and project or folder identifiers. In the current implementation these values are written to that language's replicated data and are not stored as private secrets. Other people with access to the language data may be able to read them. Do not enter credentials unless you accept that exposure; use revocable, least-privilege credentials and rotate any credentials that may have been exposed.
  • Technical and network information: the peer-to-peer network necessarily processes network connection information, including IP addresses, to establish connections. The desktop app also writes worker output to a local log file. The app's current code does not include an advertising or analytics service that reports app activity to the publisher.

3. Translation searches and other services

When you search for a word using the app's Translate feature, the searched text is sent to Google Cloud Translation, Yandex Cloud Translate, and the Burlang translation API (burlang.ru) to request results. Google and Yandex requests use the credentials configured for the selected language; a request may fail if valid credentials have not been configured. There is no separate per-provider toggle in the app. These providers receive the submitted text and may process connection and request information under their own privacy terms.

The peer-to-peer networking libraries connect participating devices without the app's publisher acting as a content server. Other peers and the networking infrastructure may process connection metadata, including IP addresses. Downloading the app through an app store is also subject to that store's own privacy terms.

4. Camera and selected files

The Android app requests camera access to scan a pairing QR code. On desktop, camera access is requested for video-only camera use by the QR scanner. The app does not use the camera to record or upload photos or video. You choose images and videos through the device's file-selection interface when adding an avatar or contribution; those files are shared as described above.

5. How information is used and shared

The app uses information to operate profiles, pair devices, replicate language data, display contributions, and request translation results. Information is shared as follows:

  • Profile and account data are replicated to authorized paired devices.
  • Language data and contributions are replicated to peers participating in that language. Anyone who obtains a language key and joins may be able to access its replicated data.
  • Translation search text is sent to the translation providers described above.
  • Connection metadata is processed by the peer-to-peer network to establish connections.

The publisher does not receive a copy of app content through a publisher-run central service in the current implementation. The publisher cannot control how other participants use information they can access.

6. Storage, security, and retention

App data is stored locally on your device and in peer replicas. Peer connections use encrypted transport, but this does not make content confidential from the peers that receive it. Protect your device and only pair devices you trust.

Data on this device remains in its local app storage until it is removed by the app, the operating system, or you clear the app's data. Uninstalling the app may not remove all app data, depending on the platform. Android system backups may include app data depending on your device and account settings. Revoking a paired device causes that device to clear its local app data when it receives the revocation. It does not erase copies already replicated to other devices or backups.

Some data is maintained in append-only replicated records. Editing a profile or removing a contribution from the app's current view may not remove earlier records or copies held by other peers. The publisher cannot guarantee deletion from devices or backups controlled by other participants.

7. Your choices

You may choose a nickname and whether to add an avatar, avoid submitting personal or sensitive information in public language contributions, and avoid using translation search when you do not want the search text sent to the translation providers. You can ask a language administrator to remove contributions where the app provides that capability, but removal may not delete replicated history or copies on other devices.

8. Children and international use

The app is a general language-collaboration tool and does not ask users to provide a date of birth. Peer-to-peer connections and third-party translation services may route or process information in countries other than the one where you live. Consider this before sharing personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

The publisher may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is published.
